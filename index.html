1.2

The Three States of Flow

Active. Passive. Neutral. You'll be in all three on the way to launching, and most people forget the third one is even an option.

Visual Principle · the framework for the whole launch

Output what you know · Consume what you don't · Receive what no one can teach you

⚡

Active · output

You're creating, posting, recording, building gems, going live. Outputting based on what you already know. The visible work, but it can't be all of it.

📥

Passive · consume

Watching trainings, reading, taking courses, sitting in other people's communities. Launching means a lot of time here, and that's correct.

🌌

Neutral · receive

The state most people skip. Nothing in, nothing out. Walks, showers, sleep, silence. The ideas no one can give you arrive here. Schedule it.

You can spend time, or you can spend money. There's no one right way, only your way and your consistency.

Employee Rhythm vs Entrepreneur Rhythm

Both rhythms work. Both can support a life. But they look completely different on the calendar, and confusing one for the other is what kills most launches at month four.

Visual Principle · the rhythm shift you'll need to survive

Same time. Same effort. Wildly different shape.

work · paid · work · paid
predictable · capped · low risk · trade time for money
🌊
Entrepreneur
work · work · work · work · work · work · work · paid · work · work · work · paid
months that feel dry, then weeks that pay for the year

Two more rules that pair with this:

  • Don't charge what you haven't paid for. If you've never paid for high-ticket coaching yourself, start with a low-ticket community. The energy of being able to receive a price is what makes you able to charge it.

      Visual Principle · the three parts of the same story

      Your niche is the deserted island. Your community is the treasure map. Your gem is the specific, tangible thing they came for.

      The Deserted Island

      This is your niche. The unique territory you operate in. It's not a marketing slogan, it's the actual world you live in and want to bring people to.

      4.2

      eBooks, PDFs, downloads, freebies, that whole era is over. Your gem is an artifact, an interactive hosted experience you build with Claude. They click a link, they use it, they get a result. No download. No printer. No "I'll get to it later" pile.

      An artifact does something. A PDF just sits there.

      B.3

      The Budget & Targeting Framework

      Forget what you've read about ads. The setup is simple: one Sales Campaign, one ad set, three ads, location-only targeting. Spend depends on what your community costs to join.

      The structure · this never changes

      One Sales Campaign. One ad set. Three ads inside.

      🎯

      Campaign type

      Always a Sales Campaign in Meta Ads Manager. Not Traffic. Not Awareness. Not Engagement. Sales, every single time. Every test is a brand new campaign, never a duplicate.

      ⚙️

      Optimization event

      Freemium community → optimize for Registration.
      Paid community → optimize for Purchase.
      The wrong event tells Meta to send the wrong people. Get this right or nothing else matters.

      🌍

      Targeting

      Location only. Tier 1 countries. United States, Canada, Australia, United Kingdom, the big four. No interests, no lookalikes, no detailed audiences. Let the optimization event decide who.

      The math · budget is set by what your community costs

      $10/day per campaign for 72 hours, or 2–3x your front-end offer.

      🆓

      Free community

      Budget: $10/day for 72 hours.
      Success threshold: cost per registration under $2.50. Anything above $2.50 means the creative or the bridge isn't landing yet, iterate and run round two.

      💰

      Paid community

      Budget: 2–3x your front-end offer per day. $8 tier? Spend $16–$24/day. Higher tier? Higher spend.
      Success threshold: cost per purchase at or under your tier price.

      📈

      Then iterate

      After 72 hours, look at the data. Kill what's bleeding, scale what's hitting, swap creatives on what's in the middle. Build fast and in public. Share what worked, what didn't. That transparency is its own visibility loop.

      The first round of ads tells you what people want, not how good you are. Read the data without ego.
